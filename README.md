California Laundromat, LLC is a veteran-owned wash and fold pickup and delivery business in Mission Valley, San Diego.
Customers leave a bag at the door. The shop collects it, washes and dries it, folds it by hand, and returns it to the same spot.
Wash and fold starts at $2.50 a pound. Delivery is $0.77 a mile and never more than $6, inside an 18-mile service area.
Pickups can be booked online any time, with turnaround from 12 hours through a weekly Tuesday-to-Thursday subscription.
